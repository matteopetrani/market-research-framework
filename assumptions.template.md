# [Product Name] - Product Assumptions

**Instructions:** Copy this file to `assumptions.md` and customize it for your specific product and market.

This document contains key hypotheses about your product concept. These assumptions will be tested and validated through research.

## Market Assumptions

### 1. Market Demand
**Assumption:** [Your target audience] represents a cohesive, addressable market segment.

**Why this matters:** Validates the core target audience exists and is identifiable.

**Validation criteria:**
- Evidence of [specific interests/needs] in [target communities]
- Size of the addressable market segment
- User behavior patterns indicating this is a distinct cohort

---

### 2. Market Size
**Assumption:** The [your niche] segment is large enough to support a viable business (>100K potential users).

**Why this matters:** Determines if the market is worth pursuing.

**Validation criteria:**
- TAM/SAM/SOM calculations
- Growth trends in [your category] + [your subcategory] categories
- Competitor user bases in adjacent segments

---

## Competitive Assumptions

### 3. Competitive Gaps
**Assumption:** Existing [category] apps lack [your key differentiation feature].

**Why this matters:** Validates the key differentiation opportunity.

**Validation criteria:**
- Competitor feature analysis shows limited [differentiation feature]
- User complaints about missing [specific capabilities]
- No clear market leader in this niche

---

### 4. [Differentiation Strategy]
**Assumption:** Users want [broad approach] rather than [narrow approach].

**Why this matters:** Informs [content/feature] strategy (broad vs. focused).

**Validation criteria:**

- Competitors focusing on [narrow approach] show demand for alternatives
- Evidence of [multi-faceted communities]
